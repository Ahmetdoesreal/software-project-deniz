\section{Purpose}

This document is a contributor-focused copy of the project Software
Requirements Specification. It extracts the requirements most relevant to the
recent implementation areas and is intended to be edited for an individual
project report. The full-project SRS remains the authoritative project-wide
requirements document.

\section{Contributor Scope}

\begin{longtable}{L{0.26\textwidth} L{0.64\textwidth}}
\textbf{Area} & \textbf{Description} \\
\hline
Incident rules and titlebar matching & Definition-based rules for focused-window/titlebar incidents, whitelist/warning/blacklist behavior, Save as Rule logic, reusable contains patterns, and browser-title handling. \\
Process executable matching & Wildcard-style executable matching for process definitions and blacklist behavior, including desktop app variants. \\
Dashboard UX stability & Full-row hover and non-disruptive refresh for client, incident, process database, and incident rule lists. \\
Auth validation & Temporary CATS/AD disable with admin-managed validation and approval/denial flow. \\
Reconnect and buffering & Keeping local logging active during disconnects and preserving incident/evidence buffers. \\
Exam files and folders & Safe extraction of exam materials to the student Desktop Exam folder and UI folder information. \\
Projector frontend & Read-only projection-safe notification page with separated HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. \\
Offline installer hardening & Shared all-users install layout, shared venv, integrity manifest, package compatibility checks, and safer permissions. \\
\end{longtable}

\section{Functional Requirements}

\subsection{Incident Rules and Titlebar Matching}

\begin{longtable}{L{0.18\textwidth} L{0.47\textwidth} L{0.25\textwidth}}
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Requirement} & \textbf{Acceptance Criteria} \\
\hline
MY-FR-INC-001 & The system shall represent titlebar and incident behavior as reusable incident rules. & Rules include status, match mode, event type, title patterns, process restrictions, priority, and actions. \\
MY-FR-INC-002 & Whitelist rules shall suppress matching candidates before warning or blacklist rules. & Approved browser titles can suppress focused-window incidents. \\
MY-FR-INC-003 & Saved titlebar rules shall prefer reusable contains patterns. & A WhatsApp title can save a pattern such as \texttt{whatsapp}. \\
MY-FR-INC-004 & Title normalization shall handle Unicode and invisible/control characters. & Browser titles with unusual spacing do not stop matching or incident processing. \\
MY-FR-INC-005 & Incident History shall provide Save as Rule behavior. & Selected incidents prefill editable rule decisions. \\
MY-FR-INC-006 & Incident-rule actions shall be saved and applied where context allows. & Ban, kick, pause, and kill-pid actions are context-checked. \\
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Process and Executable Matching}

\begin{longtable}{L{0.18\textwidth} L{0.47\textwidth} L{0.25\textwidth}}
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Requirement} & \textbf{Acceptance Criteria} \\
\hline
MY-FR-PROC-001 & Process matching shall support wildcard-style executable patterns. & One entry can match related executable variants. \\
MY-FR-PROC-002 & Process definition behavior shall remain backward compatible with exact and contains matching. & Existing definitions and blacklist entries continue to work. \\
MY-FR-PROC-003 & Process rules shall integrate with incidents and actions. & Matched processes open incidents and may trigger configured actions. \\
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Dashboard UX Stability}

\begin{longtable}{L{0.18\textwidth} L{0.47\textwidth} L{0.25\textwidth}}
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Requirement} & \textbf{Acceptance Criteria} \\
\hline
MY-FR-UI-001 & Tk and Qt lists shall highlight the full row on hover. & Hover covers all visible cells, not only the current cell. \\
MY-FR-UI-002 & Live dashboard updates shall avoid full rebuilds when row identity and order have not changed. & Timer and state updates modify row values in place. \\
MY-FR-UI-003 & Scroll, horizontal scroll, focus, and selection shall be preserved during unavoidable rebuilds. & User scrolling does not reset during normal updates. \\
MY-FR-UI-004 & Sorting and filtering shall remain available. & Explicit user sorting and filtering continue to work. \\
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Auth Validation and Temporary Disable}

\begin{longtable}{L{0.18\textwidth} L{0.47\textwidth} L{0.25\textwidth}}
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Requirement} & \textbf{Acceptance Criteria} \\
\hline
MY-FR-AUTH-001 & The server shall expose current auth requirements before login. & \texttt{/auth/status} reports CATS/AD flags, expiry, allowed-user status, validation status, and reason. \\
MY-FR-AUTH-002 & Temporarily disabled auth shall not automatically admit a user. & Matching attempts enter admin validation state. \\
MY-FR-AUTH-003 & Admins shall approve or deny pending auth validation requests. & Commands list, approve, and deny pending requests. \\
MY-FR-AUTH-004 & Client-side preflight shall reflect server auth status. & Pending validation is shown or waited for instead of misreporting normal auth. \\
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Reconnect, Buffering, Exam Files, Projector, and Installer}

\begin{longtable}{L{0.18\textwidth} L{0.47\textwidth} L{0.25\textwidth}}
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Requirement} & \textbf{Acceptance Criteria} \\
\hline
MY-FR-REC-001 & A WebSocket disconnect shall not stop local monitoring or logs. & Process, focus, idle, hardware, exam-state, GUI, replay, and incident components remain active. \\
MY-FR-REC-002 & Incidents generated while disconnected shall be queued persistently. & Buffered incidents include sequence, queued time, and buffered flag. \\
MY-FR-FILE-001 & Downloaded exam materials shall be safely extracted to Desktop Exam folder. & ZIP traversal and unsafe paths are rejected. \\
MY-FR-FILE-002 & Client and server UIs shall expose useful folder information. & Exam Folder and selected-user folder info are available. \\
MY-FR-PROJ-001 & The server shall provide a read-only projector page. & The page has no mutating controls and no private data. \\
MY-FR-PROJ-002 & Projector frontend files shall be separated. & HTML, CSS, and JavaScript are served separately. \\
MY-FR-INS-001 & The installer shall use a shared virtual environment instead of global machine site-packages. & Packages install into \texttt{C:/ProgramData/May\_04\_Deniz/python\_env}. \\
MY-FR-INS-002 & Installer permissions shall separate code from runtime data. & Normal users get read/execute for code and modify access only to data. \\
MY-FR-INS-003 & The bundle shall verify file integrity when a manifest is present. & \texttt{manifest.sha256} is checked before install. \\
\end{longtable}

\section{Non-Functional Requirements}

\section{Interfaces in Contributor Scope}

\subsection{HTTP Routes}

\begin{longtable}{L{0.35\textwidth} L{0.55\textwidth}}
\textbf{Route} & \textbf{Use} \\
\hline
\texttt{/auth/status?login\_id=<id>} & Client checks auth requirements and validation state before login. \\
\texttt{/projector} & Browser opens read-only projection page. \\
\texttt{/projector/events} & Browser receives SSE state updates. \\
\texttt{/exam/files?id=<uuid>} & Client downloads exam material ZIP for safe extraction. \\
\texttt{/client/artifact?id=<uuid>} & Client uploads incident or replay evidence. \\
\end{longtable}

\subsection{CLI Commands}

\begin{longtable}{L{0.34\textwidth} L{0.56\textwidth}}
\textbf{Command} & \textbf{Use} \\
\hline
\texttt{/disablecatsauth [seconds]} & Temporarily disable CATS preflight and require admin validation. \\
\texttt{/disableadauth [seconds]} & Temporarily disable AD auth and require admin validation. \\
\texttt{/disableauth [seconds]} & Temporarily disable both auth paths. \\
\texttt{/authstatus} & Show auth bypass and validation status. \\
\texttt{/authrequests} & List validation requests. \\
\texttt{/approveauth <id> [seconds]} & Temporarily approve pending validation. \\
\texttt{/denyauth <id> [reason]} & Deny pending validation. \\
\texttt{/editincidentrules} & Open incident rule database. \\
\texttt{/applyincidentrules} & Reload and broadcast incident rules. \\
\end{longtable}

\section{Validation Plan}

\subsection{Automated Validation}

\begin{verbatim}
cd May_04_Deniz
python -m compileall -q .
python -m unittest discover -s tests
python -m pip check
\end{verbatim}

Relevant test groups include incident rules, focused-window title normalization,
client incident buffering, auth validation, projector safety, process wildcard
matching, dashboard refresh helpers, server command handling, and offline bundle
integrity checks.

\subsection{Manual Validation}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Add \texttt{whatsapp} as a contains title pattern and verify browser title variants match.
    \item Add a wildcard executable entry for WhatsApp desktop variants and verify matching.
    \item Scroll client and incident lists while timer updates arrive and verify scroll does not reset.
    \item Hover rows in Tk and Qt tables and verify full-row highlight.
    \item Run auth disable, attempt login, approve through admin validation, and verify admission.
    \item Disconnect during an exam and verify client-side logs continue growing.
    \item Generate an incident while disconnected and verify it flushes after reconnect.
    \item Download exam materials and verify safe Desktop Exam extraction.
    \item Open the projector page at low resolution and verify readability and privacy.
    \item Test offline installer behavior in a controlled VM or lab machine.
\end{itemize}
